Our Mission

Discovering and nurturing chess talents across Iran and helping enthusiasts reach their highest potential. We believe that with proper training, anyone can become a professional chess player.

History of Polaris Academy

Polaris Academy began its activities in 2010 with the aim of elevating the level of chess in Iran, in collaboration with a group of distinguished coaches and national champions. Over 15 years of continuous activity, relying on modern teaching methods and utilizing cutting-edge technologies, we have become Iran's most prestigious chess academy.

We are proud that over 5,000 students from across Iran and even neighboring countries have participated in our courses, many of whom are now prominent champions and successful chess coaches. Our educational programs are designed based on FIDE standards and are constantly updated.
